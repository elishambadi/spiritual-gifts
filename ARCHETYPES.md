# Spiritual Gift Archetypes

A talent system for recognizing and engaging people's spiritual gifts for active church work.

**The principle:** Don't recruit for a role — recruit for the family, then place by signature. A Shepherd doing evangelism will burn out; a Herald parked in facilities will too. Run every volunteer through the survey, read their **top-3 gift blend**, and place them into the family their signature points to.

There are **12 archetypes** across **3 ministry families**, mirroring how a healthy church functions:

- **REACH** (outward voice: grow & win)
- **KEEP** (inward care: retain & deepen)
- **BUILD** (operations: make it run)

Each archetype is a *gift signature* (a blend), not a single gift.

---

## Family A — REACH (Outward voice: grow & win)

### 1. The Pioneer
- **Gift Signature:** Apostleship + Faith + Leadership + Wisdom
- **Tagline:** Starts what doesn't yet exist.
- **Description:** Sees the unreached frontier, trusts God for provision, and gathers others to launch new work.
- **Fits These Roles:** Church planting, mission director, conference/senior leadership, vision-casting
- **How to Engage:** Give them a frontier, not a system to maintain.

### 2. The Herald
- **Gift Signature:** Evangelism + Faith + Exhortation
- **Tagline:** Wins hearts for the Kingdom.
- **Description:** Communicates the gospel with clarity and urgency, and calls others to respond.
- **Fits These Roles:** Public evangelism, community outreach, revival meetings, street/market ministry, personal witnessing
- **How to Engage:** Put them in front of the unchurched, not in committee rooms.

### 3. The Prophet
- **Gift Signature:** Prophecy + Discernment + Knowledge
- **Tagline:** Speaks truth the church needs to hear.
- **Description:** Proclaims God's Word boldly and distinguishes truth from error on behalf of the body.
- **Fits These Roles:** Preaching rotation, spiritual accountability, doctrinal watch, calling the church to faithfulness
- **How to Engage:** Pair them with mercy gifts so truth lands with love.

### 4. The Sage
- **Gift Signature:** Teaching + Knowledge + Wisdom
- **Tagline:** Makes truth stick and apply.
- **Description:** Gathers, clarifies, and communicates truth in a way that transforms how people live.
- **Fits These Roles:** Sabbath School, Bible study, equipping/training, curriculum, mentoring future teachers
- **How to Engage:** Hand them a classroom and a student, not a spreadsheet.

## Family B — KEEP (Inward care: retain & deepen)

### 5. The Shepherd
- **Gift Signature:** Shepherding + Mercy + Exhortation
- **Tagline:** Tends the flock one soul at a time.
- **Description:** Nurtures, cares for, and guides individuals toward spiritual maturity.
- **Fits These Roles:** Pastoral care, small-group leader, visitation, counseling, discipleship
- **How to Engage:** Give them a few people to love deeply, not a crowd to manage.

### 6. The Encourager
- **Gift Signature:** Exhortation + Mercy + Shepherding
- **Tagline:** Meets people in the valley and walks them up.
- **Description:** Comes alongside the struggling, wounded, and drifting to comfort and re-engage them.
- **Fits These Roles:** New-member assimilation, follow-up, one-on-one mentoring, hospital visits, recovery groups
- **How to Engage:** Point them at the ones drifting — they will re-engage them.

### 7. The Watchman
- **Gift Signature:** Discernment + Knowledge + Wisdom
- **Tagline:** Protects the church's truth and health.
- **Description:** Senses what is off, harmful, or counterfeit and protects the body with wisdom.
- **Fits These Roles:** Elder board, doctrinal review, prayer ministry, spiritual warfare, gatekeeping
- **How to Engage:** Let them guard the door; thank them for what they prevent.

### 8. The Comforter
- **Gift Signature:** Faith + Discernment + Mercy
- **Tagline:** Prays heaven into earth's hardest rooms.
- **Description:** Carries the burdens of others to God with confidence and compassionate presence.
- **Fits These Roles:** Intercessory prayer, crisis care, grief ministry, praying over decisions
- **How to Engage:** Give them a prayer list and the hurting; shield their quiet time.

---

## Family C — BUILD (Operations: make it run)

### 9. The Organizer
- **Gift Signature:** Administration + Leadership + Service
- **Tagline:** Turns vision into a running system.
- **Description:** Orders ideas, resources, time, and people so ministry actually happens.
- **Fits These Roles:** Church clerk, board member, event logistics, department coordination, records
- **How to Engage:** Let them run operations; don't make them the public face.

### 10. The Servant
- **Gift Signature:** Service/Helps + Mercy + Hospitality
- **Tagline:** Holds up the tent in the background.
- **Description:** Meets practical unmet needs with quiet, cheerful, behind-the-scenes faithfulness.
- **Fits These Roles:** Deacons, facilities/maintenance, community services, feeding ministries, setup/cleanup
- **How to Engage:** Give them concrete tasks and genuine thanks; never let them become invisible.

### 11. The Steward
- **Gift Signature:** Giving + Administration + Service
- **Tagline:** Fuels the mission with resources.
- **Description:** Mobilizes material resources generously and wisely to advance God's work.
- **Fits These Roles:** Stewardship dept, trust/benevolence fund, generosity campaigns, missions funding
- **How to Engage:** Give them numbers and a cause; trust their discretion.

### 12. The Host
- **Gift Signature:** Hospitality + Mercy + Service
- **Tagline:** Makes strangers feel at home.
- **Description:** Opens doors, sets tables, and makes every visitor feel valued and welcomed.
- **Fits These Roles:** Greeter team, fellowship/potluck, visitor welcome, assimilation events, guest lodging
- **How to Engage:** Put them at every doorway and every table.

## Matching Logic

In the app, an archetype match is computed from a participant's ranked gift scores:

1. Take each archetype's **signature** (primary gift weighted highest).
2. Check which signature gifts appear in the participant's **top 5 gifts**.
3. Score = sum of weights for matched gifts; **match percentage** = score / maximum possible.
4. The highest-scoring archetype is the **primary match**; the next is the **runner-up**.

The result tells leaders *where* to place a person, not just *what* they are.
